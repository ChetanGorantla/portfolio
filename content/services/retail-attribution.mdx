## Summary

I built a retail attribution backend on Python and Cloud Run. It ingests real-time speech, POS, and video data through 3 REST APIs and matches POS transactions to ad impressions within a 2-minute window, which automates ad-to-purchase attribution.

## Approach

Three input streams, each with its own job:

- **Ingestion.** 3 REST APIs on Cloud Run take in real-time speech, POS, and video data.
- **Speech.** An OpenAI NLP classifier extracts upsell intent and product mentions from the conversation.
- **Video.** A real-time YOLOv8/OpenCV pipeline uses calibrated proximity scoring to verify cashier-customer interactions.

With those signals in place, the backend matches POS transactions to ad impressions within a 2-minute window.

## The hard part

Two inputs I couldn't take at face value.

**Speech is untrusted input.** Whatever someone says ends up inside a prompt, so the extraction had to be prompt-injection resistant. The transcript is data to classify, not instructions to follow.

**Being close isn't the same as interacting.** Two people standing near each other on camera aren't necessarily talking. The proximity scoring had to be calibrated before it could be trusted to verify a cashier-customer interaction.

## Resolution

- POS transactions matched to ad impressions within a 2-minute window.
- Ad-to-purchase attribution automated end to end.
- Speech classified for upsell intent and product mentions with prompt-injection-resistant extraction. Cashier-customer interactions verified from video.
