## In short

I'm Tech Lead for Texas Convergent's company-wide Forge partnership with Family Dollar, leading engineering for 2 projects and working directly with the CIO.

## The problem

- **Out-of-stock shelves** hurt store efficiency and sell rate.
- **Pinpad skimmers** target EBT cardholders, who get less protection than credit card customers.

## What we're building

- **Shelves:** find high-risk shelves, derive fullness scores, and track the trend over time
- **Skimmers:** two-pass object detection that first locates the pinpad, then checks it for telltale characteristics
- Both are checked against hand-labeled data for accuracy

## The hard part

A real-world environment is far more constrained than a personal project. Balancing **cost, security, and feasibility** is harder than the code, and it takes communication across company stakeholders to agree on the tradeoffs.

## What I learned (so far)

Leading a team means defending its decisions, weighing outcomes on the fly, and knowing what's going on at every step. Keeping morale high when we hit problems is hard, but necessary.
