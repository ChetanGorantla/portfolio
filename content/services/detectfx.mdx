## Summary

DetectFX listens to a guitar recording, identifies which of 13 effects are on it, and recreates the tone. A trained classifier does the identifying. A set of DSP effects I built in NumPy and SciPy does the recreating.

## Approach

- **Classify.** A 13-class guitar-effect classifier, trained on the EGFxSet dataset.
- **Measure.** An 18-dimensional audio feature delta, derived from FFT analysis.
- **Recreate.** Threshold-calibrated rules map that feature delta to 9 conditional DSP effects. I built 10 DSP effects in NumPy/SciPy, covering frequency filters, feedback delay networks, and nonlinear waveshaping.
- **Store.** Storage is decoupled from compute using UUID-namespaced, per-user Supabase paths.

## The hard part

**512 MB.** Inference had to fit inside a 512 MB Heroku dyno. I got it there with staged profiling, finding where the memory went one stage at a time instead of guessing.

## Resolution

- Identifies 13 guitar effects from a recording and recreates the tone.
- Inference fits in a 512 MB Heroku dyno.
- 10 DSP effects built in NumPy/SciPy.
- Per-user storage separated with UUID-namespaced Supabase paths.
